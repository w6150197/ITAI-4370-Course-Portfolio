# Module 9: Sustainable AI and Green Communications

**Course:** ITAI 4370 — 5/6G Communications and O-RAN Networks  
**Student:** Annette Bazan

## Selected work
Energy optimization and Q-learning exercises

## Problem and learning objective
Studied how greener network operation can reduce energy demand and emissions.

## Methods, tools, and work completed
Used linear programming for a 100 kWh supply mix: 60 kWh wind, 40 kWh solar, 0 kWh grid, costing $8.80. Calculated 40,000 kg CO₂ for 200,000 kWh at 60% renewable share and a 0.5 kg/kWh grid factor. Explored base-station sleep states and a simplified Q-learning reward table.

## Results and interpretation
Learned to separate verified optimization outcomes from conceptual reinforcement-learning examples.
