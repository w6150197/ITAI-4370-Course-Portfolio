## Problem and learning objective
Discussed terminology from Merenda, Porcaro, and Iero (2020), including machine learning, neural networks, deep learning, edge computing, and IoT.

## Methods, tools, and work completed
Compared TensorFlow/Keras models on synthetic 3-class sensor data: baseline accuracy 93.35% (51.26 KB), reduced 93.40% (13.76 KB), INT8 quantized 92.45% (21.75 KB), and small student 91.25% (4.89 KB).

## Results and interpretation
Learned accuracy/size/latency tradeoffs. Quantized 0.5 ms latency was an estimate, not a device benchmark; the smaller models were not demonstrated as formal pruning or knowledge distillation.
