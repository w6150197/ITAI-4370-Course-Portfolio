**Course:** ITAI 4370 — 5/6G Communications and O-RAN Networks  
**Student:** Annette Bazan

## Selected work
Assignment 03, Lab 3 and additional exercises

## Problem and learning objective
Studied AI-driven radio resource management, self-organizing networks, predictive maintenance, quality of service, and xApps/rApps.

## Methods, tools, and work completed
Trained a Random Forest on synthetic hourly traffic data. The chronological test set yielded MSE 34.64 and R² 0.893; the one-hour lag was the strongest feature. Additional exercises explored Isolation Forest/DBSCAN anomalies and K-Means customer segmentation.

## Results and interpretation
Learned the importance of time-aware evaluation and checking feature leakage: centered rolling features can use future observations.
