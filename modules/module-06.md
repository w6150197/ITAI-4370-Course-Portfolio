Lab 4: Network Traffic Prediction Using Time Series Analysis

## Problem and learning objective
Examined seasonal traffic variation and predictive network planning.

## Methods, tools, and work completed
Generated 8,760 synthetic hourly observations. Mean traffic was 167.354 Mbps and standard deviation 47.938 Mbps. Used time-series visualization, seasonal decomposition, an ADF test (statistic -12.832, displayed p-value 0.000000), and ARIMA(2,1,2).

## Results and interpretation
Learned how traffic trends and seasonality inform capacity planning. The provided lab evidence did not establish a final ARIMA forecast error.
